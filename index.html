      Drag any device, beaker, bottle or tool to arrange the bench. Click the faucet for water; hold a beaker or cylinder and click it to fill. Hold a sensor or tool and click any drawer or the wall cabinet to put it away. The VERNIER SENSORS drawer holds temperature, pH, conductivity, gas pressure and drop-counter sensors, a dropper and the SpectroVis spectrophotometer; their readings show on the LabQuest. The CO₂ extinguisher puts out fires. Measure liquids in a cylinder, weigh solids in a boat, weigh anything on the balance, insulate a beaker with the foam cups, stir, and use tongs for hot glass. The shower and eyewash work too. Click a bottle in the fume hood to pick it up, then click a beaker to pour. Pick up a beaker and click another beaker to combine them, the hot plate to heat it, the red waste container to dispose of it, or the sink to pour it away.
